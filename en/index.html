IP-SYMCON

All your building technology — in images instead of lists

BDS talks to IP-Symcon. Every area gets its own image tile. It shows at once what is running and what has stopped: green is fine, red is a fault.

  • Own image collection: ventilation, light, alarm system, revolving doors, irrigation and more
  • Fault tile counts everything pending — otherwise it says “OK”
  • Arm and disarm the alarm system right on the tile
  • Suppress faults for a while, e.g. escalator under maintenance
  • Ready-made templates and a ticket from any fault with one tap

SECURITY

Your data stays with you.

With the on-site system, tickets, plans, photos and user accounts are stored only on the BDS computer in your building. Access on the go is encrypted all the way and is only opened on your premises.

Your system establishes the connection outwards by itself. No port needs to be opened in your firewall.

Data only on site

Backups stay with you too. The data centre holds no content of your system.

Encrypted all the way to you

Your own certificate for your system, with its own encrypted tunnel underneath. Your system achieves the top SSL Labs rating: A+.

Second factor for every account

Sign-in with a code from an authenticator app or a link by e-mail. After several failed attempts, access is locked.

Building technology sealed off

The interfaces to IP-Symcon are blocked from outside. Switching is only possible after sign-in and with the right permission.

Signed updates only

Your system accepts only digitally signed software versions, no older ones, and installs them only at the push of a button.

Works without internet

If the line goes down, BDS keeps working on site. Only access on the go is unavailable.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What customers want to know beforehand

Can I connect my existing systems?

Yes, via IP-Symcon: KNX, HomeMatic and many other bus systems. Whatever Symcon knows, you control and monitor in BDS. Cameras also work without Symcon.

Do I need my own server?

Not necessarily. Large properties usually choose the server in their own building, service providers and smaller properties the cloud in Germany. Both are operated the same way.

Do all employees have to install the app?

No. Cleaning staff, contractors and customers can also report via QR code without the app, in 6 languages.

I am missing a feature. What then?

Then we build the module with you: first a clickable draft, then we agree on it, then we build it in, in the app and the browser.
